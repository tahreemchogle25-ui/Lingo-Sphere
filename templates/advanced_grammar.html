01

Complex Sentence Structure

What is a complex sentence?
A complex sentence contains one independent clause and at least one dependent clause.

Simple Ideas

The research was successful.
The researchers used a new method.
→

Combined Idea

The research was successful because the researchers used a new method.
Common subordinating conjunctions
because • although • while • since • when • if • unless • whereas
02

Active & Passive Voice

Active Voice
The researcher conducted the experiment.

Active

The researcher conducted the experiment.
→

Passive

The experiment was conducted by the researcher.
Academic writing tip
Passive voice is often used when the action or result is more important than the person performing the action.
03

Subject–Verb Agreement

Rule:
A singular subject generally takes a singular verb, while a plural subject takes a plural verb.

Correct ✓

The student works on the project.
VS

Incorrect ✕

The student work on the project.
