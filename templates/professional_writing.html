✨ Speaking Tips

Small improvements can make your communication more professional.

1
Speak slowly enough for the listener to understand each important point.
2
Avoid unnecessary filler words such as "umm", "like" and "you know".
3
Use short pauses between important ideas instead of speaking continuously.
4
Maintain confident body language and appropriate eye contact during presentations.

📊 Build a Strong Presentation

A professional presentation should have a clear structure. Follow these four stages.

1

Introduction

Introduce yourself and clearly state the topic of your presentation.

2

Main Points

Present your important ideas in a logical and organized sequence.

3

Evidence

Support your ideas with examples, facts or relevant explanations.

4

Conclusion

Summarize the main points and finish with a clear final statement.

💬 Academic Conversation

Example of a professional classroom discussion.

PROFESSOR
What do you think are the major benefits of online learning?
STUDENT
In my opinion, online learning provides greater flexibility and allows students to access educational resources from different locations.
PROFESSOR
That is an interesting point. Could you give an example?
STUDENT
For example, students can attend recorded lectures and review difficult concepts according to their own learning pace.

Professional Speaking Phrases

Use these expressions instead of informal or unclear language.

"In my opinion..." Useful when expressing your personal viewpoint in a discussion.
"I would like to point out..." Useful when introducing an important idea.
"To clarify..." Useful when explaining something more clearly.
"For example..." Useful when providing supporting evidence.
"However..." Useful when presenting a contrast or different viewpoint.
"To conclude..." Useful when introducing the final part of a presentation.
