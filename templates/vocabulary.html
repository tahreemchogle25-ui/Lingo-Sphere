Essential Vocabulary

Learn the meaning, pronunciation and practical usage of each word.

Analyze
VERB

To examine information carefully in order to understand its parts, meaning or relationship.

Example: Students analyze the results of the experiment.
Evaluate
VERB

To judge the quality, value or effectiveness of something using evidence or criteria.

Example: Researchers evaluate different solutions.
Significant
ADJECTIVE

Important or meaningful enough to have a noticeable effect or influence.

Example: The study showed a significant improvement.
Evidence
NOUN

Facts or information used to support an idea, argument or conclusion.

Example: The researcher provided evidence for the claim.
Approach
NOUN

A particular method or way of dealing with a problem or situation.

Example: This approach improves student engagement.
Establish
VERB

To prove, demonstrate or successfully create something.

Example: The research establishes a clear relationship.
