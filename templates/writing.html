📧 Formal Email Writing

Learn the structure of a professional academic email.

Correct Structure

A formal email should be clear, polite and properly structured.

1
Use a clear and meaningful subject line.
2
Begin with an appropriate greeting.
3
State the purpose clearly in the first paragraph.
4
End with a polite closing statement.

Example Email

Example of writing to a college professor.

Subject: Request for Assignment Submission Extension

Dear Professor,

I am writing to request a short extension for submitting the assignment. Due to an unexpected academic commitment, I require additional time to complete the work properly.

I would be grateful if you could allow me to submit the assignment by the revised date.

Thank you for your understanding.

Sincerely,
Tahreem

🔄 Paraphrasing Practice

Paraphrasing means expressing the same idea using different words and sentence structures without changing the original meaning.

ORIGINAL Technology provides students with access to educational resources from almost anywhere.
PARAPHRASED Digital technology allows learners to obtain educational materials regardless of their location.
